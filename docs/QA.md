## Not verified / limitations

An alternative browser launch was attempted; its Firefox executable is absent.
Firefox/WebKit behavior, physical phones, OS-level minimize/restore, assistive
technology use, independent first-time players and human aesthetic acceptance
are **Not Verified**. No such approval is claimed. SVG/DOM uses the approved
pose-based and textual-DAG fallbacks; mobile overview names are additionally
available through the readable resident strip and inspectors, with pan/zoom.
Fictional authored schedules are finite; this is not open social AI.
